WHAT DAN DOES AND WHAT WE DECIDE
Dan runs KuCoin Futures Grid bots in the KuCoin app (no public grid-bot API). Our system decides everything about each bot and Dan enters the form: which coin, the direction, the entry, the margin split (used margin plus any reserve), the trading range, the grid count, the stop-loss outside the range, and when to stop a bot and replace it with another. Every bot uses 1,000 USDT of margin. Objective: completed grids per hour, with every completed grid paying +1 USDT after fees. A bot is never stopped for a profit target. Price moving against us INSIDE the range is wanted; that is how grids complete. Price leaving the range, or a coin that stops completing grids, is what triggers a replacement. We must never be liquidated: the liquidation price must always sit outside the range with a safety buffer, and the system must know every trade done and every profit at all times.

THE HOURLY PIPELINE
1. SCAN every active KuCoin USDT perpetual as a leverage grid candidate. Filters: 24h volume ≥ 500K USDT, spread ≤ 0.1 %, listing age ≥ 7 days, funding within ±0.1 % per 8h, and top-of-book depth ≥ one grid's notional. Score = expected completed grids per hour: the number of step-sized crossings the last 24h and last 4h of 1-minute candles would have produced using that coin's own setup from step 3. Also reproduce KuCoin's Volatility column, document the definition, and verify it against the 23 symbols in the fixture within a tolerance.
2. RADAR: keep the best 5 with score, direction, full setup and a one-line reason. Persist hourly snapshots so the radar history is auditable. The radar always contains coins that are not already running.
3. SETUP for each radar coin, all mandatory, in this order. Margin is fixed at 1,000 USDT per bot.
   a. DIRECTION chosen by the system from the 4h and 24h trend (EMA slope, higher-highs/lower-lows), price position inside the 24h and 7d range, and funding sign. Near the bottom of the range or trending up → Long. Near the top or trending down → Short. Ranging with no clear trend → Neutral. Small score margin → Neutral. Output direction, score and reason.
   b. ENTRY: the price and moment to start. Long: inside the lower half of the range, never at a fresh high. Short: the mirror. Neutral: near the middle of the range. If the current price does not qualify, output a trigger price and wait.
   c. RANGE LIMITS (low, high): the band where the bot trades and where price may move against us freely. Long: about −15 % / +40 % from entry, Short the mirror, Neutral symmetric; then clip to recent structure (7d low/high, ATR multiples) and report the clipped values.
   d. LEVERAGE, GRID COUNT and per-grid notional from 1,000 USDT × leverage such that profit per completed grid = +1.0 USDT after 0.06 % fees per fill. Leverage allowed 3x–6x; choose the highest leverage whose liquidation price stays outside the range by a safety buffer (rule below). If the step is wider than the coin's typical 1-minute movement, reduce the grid count only as far as +1 USDT still holds. A coin whose setup cannot reach +1 USDT per grid with a safe liquidation is rejected and drops off the radar with the reason.
   e. LIQUIDATION AND RESERVE: compute the estimated liquidation price with the full position loaded at the far edge of the range. It must be at least 10 % beyond the range edge on the losing side (Long: below low; Short: above high; Neutral: both). If it is not, first add reserve margin (split the 1,000 USDT into used margin plus reserve, as KuCoin's Reserved Margin field), then lower leverage, then narrow the range on the losing side, in that order, until the buffer holds. Report used margin, reserve, liquidation price and the buffer in range percent.
   f. STOP-LOSS: a stop price outside the range on the losing side, between the range edge and the liquidation price, at which the bot is closed if price breaks out. Report it as the KuCoin stop-loss field.
   g. Output the exact KuCoin form: pair, direction, leverage, margin used, reserved margin, entry/trigger price, low, high, grids, stop-loss, plus KuCoin's preview numbers: profit per grid after fees, estimated liquidation price, order count.
4. RUN the best 2 radar coins. Dan enters them in the app and maintains a small JSON of running bots (pair, direction, leverage, margin, reserve, entry, low, high, grids, stop-loss, start time). The system reads it every hour.
5. TRACK every running bot from the 1-minute candles: every grid buy and sell that would have executed, completed grids per hour over the last 1h, 6h and 24h, grid profit, open position and average entry, unrealized PnL at the current price, funding paid, distance from price to each range edge, to the stop-loss and to the liquidation price. Persist this ledger per bot and per hour. Any hour where price is within 5 % of the liquidation price is flagged as an emergency with the action to take (add reserve or stop).
6. REPLACE, hourly, per running bot. "Not enough grids" is the trigger. If the 6h realized rate is below a minimum (configurable; default half of the expected rate that justified starting the bot, never below 2 grids per hour) for M consecutive hours (default 2), or the price has left the range, or the stop-loss was hit, or the direction score has flipped and held for M hours, the bot is marked for replacement. The replacement is the highest-ranked radar coin not already running whose expected grids per hour exceeds the failing bot's realized rate by a factor (default 2×); go down the radar in order until one qualifies; if none qualifies, keep the bot and say so. A direction flip may propose the same coin with a new direction. Always print the switch cost (unrealized PnL plus fees to close) next to every replace signal; never hide it. After a replacement, refresh the radar.
