Questions for the next audit

  • Which entry checks eliminate weak signals without removing profitable opportunities?
  • Do positions with additional buys recover after costs, or accumulate larger losses?
  • Are losses concentrated in particular coins, exit reasons or market periods?
  • Does the liquidation filter improve net results and drawdown over a meaningful sample?
