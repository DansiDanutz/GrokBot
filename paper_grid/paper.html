Entry rules · how a bot qualifies

New-entry rules: At least 12 configured grids inside confirmed support / resistance only, with estimated leveraged return greater than 1% per completed grid after both 0.06% fill fees at 5×. Entry targets allow one-order rounding tolerance: Long 40% BUY / 60% SELL; Short 60% BUY / 40% SELL; Neutral 50% / 50% across both books. These are pending-order proportions, not guaranteed profits. Existing bots keep their original ranges and orders; the current split changes as fills occur.

    How the score is calculated

    Score / 100 = component points minus penalties, clamped between 0 and 100. This is a fixed ranking formula, not a probability of profit. The score does not change the bot’s exit rules.

    Earned points · maximum 100

    • Oscillation · 30: expected grids/hour × 1.5; maximum 30 at 20 grids/hour.
    • Trend clarity · 20: LONG/SHORT = 20; TURNING-UP/DOWN = 14; NEUTRAL in the middle 25–75% of its 7-day range = 16, otherwise 6. LONG/SHORT means the daily and 4-hour trends agree.
    • Turnover · 10: linear from 0 to 10 points as 24-hour turnover rises from 3M to 30M USDT; capped at 10.
    • Spread · 5: 5 points at or below 0.05%; falls linearly to 0 at 0.15%.
    • Room · 15: distance to the nearest grid-range edge ÷ ATR4h × 7.5; capped at 15. Outside the range earns 0.
    • Funding · 10: 10 when funding favours the direction (negative for Long/Turning-up, positive for Short/Turning-down); otherwise 5 when the absolute rate is below 0.01%, else 0. Neutral earns 5 or 0 under that same small-rate test.
    • Stability · 10: ATR1h ÷ (ATR4h / 2) between 0.6 and 1.4 earns 10; otherwise 4. ATR measures typical price movement.

    Penalties

    • Mover risk −15: absolute 24-hour price change exceeds 30%.
    • Young listing −10: listed for fewer than 14 days.
    • Stale data −20: snapshot older than 60 minutes.
    • Major low yield −10: BTC, ETH or SOL.

    How a coin earns a Core seat

    On a fresh start, the five highest-scoring qualifying coins form Core. A Bench coin needs at least 10 more points than the lowest Core coin, which must have held its seat for at least two hours. At most one score-based swap occurs per hourly scan. Two missed scans remove a Core coin regardless.

    A watchlist swap does not close an existing bot. Only Core coins can open new bots, subject to capacity, direction slots and the other entry rules.
